Content model

One record for each campus, person, figure, program and story, written once and placed on any site by reference.

What repeats today

  1. People became pages

    www publishes 63 /administration/ pages, one per staff member, that only feed lists. The sitemap cuts them and moves 20 leadership profiles into one profile template.

  2. Programs became pages

    53 /programs/ pages on www exist to fill the AI program list. They stay as card data and stop publishing as pages. Entrepreneurship's plugin made 142 partner, 29 program and 15 industry pages the same way.

  3. Campuses exist twice

    northeastern.edu/campuses/<city>/ pages repeat the city sites, and the 14-campus list sits on every Batch 1 page.

  4. Figures are typed by hand

    The 97% employment figure appears on three Batch 1 pages and “14 campuses” on two, each typed separately, so a new figure means finding every copy.

One hub, every site

Each thing is written once, by the team that owns it. Sites and pages read it by reference.

The records

Eleven kinds of record cover what repeats across the Batch 1 sites and External Affairs. Each one has a design-system component that shows it.

  • Campus

    Name, city, country, coordinates, photograph, local site, contacts, emergency numbers

    Kept today
    nu_campuses on northeastern.edu, again on each city site
    Shown by
    GusGlobe, GlobalFooter, FactSheet

  • Person

    Name, title, unit, photograph, short and long bio, profile link

    Kept today
    nu_administration and nu_trustees on www, research.northeastern.edu/people, college directories, office team pages
    Shown by
    PersonCard, FeaturedPerson, ProfileHeader, PortraitWall, DirectoryGrid

  • Figure

    Number, the sentence it finishes, source link, as-of date, owner

    Kept today
    Typed by hand into each page
    Shown by
    Stat, StatBand, ProofPoint

  • Program

    Name, degree, college, campuses, format, link to apply

    Kept today
    nu_programs on www, college sites, graduate admissions
    Shown by
    ProgramCard, Finder, PathwayCards

  • Story

    Headline, date, photograph, excerpt, link, topics

    Kept today
    Northeastern Global News (WordPress REST)
    Shown by
    StoryCard, CardGrid

  • Event

    Title, date, campus, host unit, registration link

    Kept today
    Several events calendars, which also auto-generate pages
    Shown by
    EventCard, EventList, EventSeries

  • Organization

    Name, kind (employer, partner, startup), locations, logo, link

    Kept today
    Co-op employer lists, entrepreneurship partner and portfolio pages, research partner pages
    Shown by
    LogoWall, PartnerCases, GusGlobe

  • Unit

    Name, kind (college, office, institute, center), parent, campuses, contacts, site

    Kept today
    nu_colleges on www, research institute and center pages, office pages
    Shown by
    OfficeProfile, ContactBlock, PathwayList

  • Media

    Photograph or film, credit, alt text, focal point, rights

    Kept today
    Each site's own media library
    Shown by
    Image, Video, Figure

  • Route

    URL, verdict (keep, move, merge, cut), destination, redirect

    Kept today
    The sitemap's verdicts, today only in the review

  • Navigation

    Global header, footer and Explore menus

    Kept today
    Copied into every site's theme
    Shown by
    GlobalHeader, GlobalFooter, MegaMenu

How records connect

Rules

  1. One record, one owner

    Each kind of record has the team that keeps it; only they edit it, and every site reads the same copy.

  2. Pages place records, never paste them

    A page asks for a campus, a leader or three stories by reference. Change the record and every page that shows it follows.

  3. Every figure carries its source and date

    A figure can't go on a page without a source link and an as-of date, so stale numbers are easy to find.

  4. Stories stay on Northeastern Global News

    Sites pull stories from the newsroom by topic or by hand; nothing is re-posted.

  5. Old URLs are records too

    Every retired page keeps a route to where its content went, so links from search and email keep working.

Already at work in staging

The Batch 1 pages are built this way now: stories come from Northeastern Global News, the globe's co-op employers from one list, and each page's copy from one capture of the live page. Change the source and every page that shows it rebuilds.
