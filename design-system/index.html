Leave comments

  • Comment anywhere: on a sheet, a single variant, a prop row.
  • Name the change you want and where it applies (this component, or the whole system).
  • Decisions marked “departs from 22a” are the ones most worth a look.

Decisions to review

Northeastern Design System · Guide · Principlesv0.1 · 2026-11-16

Night and Canvas

22a is a screening: a dark room, thin display type at festival scale, photographs with a vignette, hairline rails, red only on the line that says now. A university site is also a reading room and a front desk. The system keeps 22a's voice and gives it two grounds.

Night
Heroes, feature rows, spotlights, closers, the global header
Canvas
Program detail, directories, tables, forms, long reading
Never
Both grounds inside one component

Persuade

Unit homepages, campaigns, program landings. 22a at full commitment: film, spotlight rows, the closer.

Read and operate

Detail pages, stories, policies; directories, finders, forms, schedules. Measure and rhythm for reading; precision, density and visible state for doing.

Atoms

  • One element, one job
  • Read only tokens
  • Icon, Button, Heading, Image, Input…

Molecules

  • Atoms working as one interaction
  • Field = Label + Input + Hint + ErrorText
  • No new visual values

Organisms

  • One feature area of a page
  • Compose molecules and atoms
  • GlobalHeader, Finder, StoryRail, Form

Templates

  • Page structures with named slots
  • No real data, no fetching
  • Unit home, Detail, Finder, Story

Presentational

  • Content arrives as props and slots
  • Actions leave as named events
  • Every sheet renders with mock content and no backend

Composition over flags

  • A structurally new variant is a new molecule
  • Cards are cards only when the whole card is one link
  • Content has no chrome: rows are ruled, not boxed

Tokens for every value

  • Components read roles, never brand values
  • A dark band is data-mode="dark"
  • Change a token, every component follows

Defects the system fixes by design

Rules

  • Read colour through roles. A dark band is data-mode="dark" on its section.
  • Primary action: ink on Canvas turning red on hover; canvas on Night turning gray-300.
  • Red text or a bare red line on Night: 2.99:1 on gray-900. Red there is a fill under white text (5.9:1) or a mark with a canvas ring.
  • Red as a section background, heading colour or decorative rule.
  • Unit palettes. Units may not have independent colours (brand).
  • Status colours (green, amber, blue) are extrapolated: the brand defines none. They always travel with an icon and a word.

Contrast, measured

  • Body on Canvas 17.7:1 · on Night 17.7:1
  • Muted on Canvas 5.5:1 · on Night 7.0:1
  • White on NU Red 5.9:1
  • NU Red on Canvas 5.4:1 (text allowed)
  • Field borders 5.5:1 · 7.0:1

Space and layout

An 8-point scale with a 4px half step, a 12-column grid inside a 1280px wrap, and three breakpoints. Full-bleed rows start on the content edge, as 22a's story rows do.

12 columns

Splits

  • 7 / 5 and 5 / 7: copy beside media
  • 4 / 8: heading beside a list or table
  • 8 / 4: reading column with section nav or key facts
  • 3 / 9: filters beside results

Vertical rhythm

  • Three section gaps, in total between two sections: section-gap-tight 64, section-gap 96, section-gap-chapter 128 (one stop less under 960)
  • Heading to content: head-gap 48 (32 under 960)
  • One owner: nu-section. Hairlines between sections are opt-in

Phones

  • Every split stacks below 960
  • Rows that scroll horizontally snap and keep the content edge
  • Wide tables scroll inside their own box
Northeastern Design System · Guide · Shape, depth and motionv0.1 · 2026-11-16

Shape, depth and motion

Square photographs, pill actions, softly rounded floating panels. Flat on Canvas; on Night, 22a's scrims and vignette carry the depth. One exponential ease-out for every movement.

Media
Square corners (brand rule, overrides 22a)
Actions
Pill 999px or circle
Panels
12px menus and popovers, 16px dialogs and glass cards
Ease
cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1) · 200 / 350 / 700 / 1200ms
none · 0photographs, video
xs · 2fields, focus
sm · 8small popovers
md · 12menus, toggletips
lg · 16dialogs, glass cards
pillbuttons, chips, tags

Canvas · flat

Structure is a hairline.

Shadow only when floating: menus, dialogs.

Night · scene

Glass over film: blur 14px, hairline edge, shadow.

Durations

  • dur-fast 200ms: hover, press, toggles
  • dur-base 350ms: menus, popovers, accordions
  • dur-slow 700ms: card rows, spotlight swaps
  • dur-scene 1200ms: hero film crossfades

Behaviour

  • Visible by default; motion only enhances
  • Exits a third faster than entrances
  • One authored moment per page (22a: the chapter change)

Reduced motion

  • Everything stills; films show their poster
  • Anything that moves on its own has a pause control
  • No count-up figures, no parallax
