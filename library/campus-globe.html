Globe

Stable
LayoutView GlobeViewOrbit GlobeOrbitHorizon GlobeHorizonTour GlobeTourChapters GlobeChaptersList CampusGlobe

A workhorse. A set of places as a ruled list of names (each a link) beside a small globe. Pointing at or focusing a name turns the globe to it in one ease of --dur-scene and marks it red with its name; pointing at or tapping a mark lights its name (aria-current). The globe never turns by itself unless tour=True (it then visits each place in turn, with a MediaControl pause, held under reduced motion). From 960px the heading and names on columns 1-5, the globe on 7-12 (--reverse swaps); phones: heading, the globe with its lower third cut, then the names. Without script the globe is a still SVG with every place marked. When to use a map, and when the NameWall list: the list where people scan by name and a picture helps them choose; a map where breadth or geography is the point, or where the photographs are weak. CampusBand puts the geography into a NameWall's heading.

What it takes

view(lon, lat)the middle of the setThe resting camera.
tourboolFalseVisit each place in turn while in view and nobody is pointing; adds the pause control.
landboolTrueEmbed the land outline once a page (False on a second globe).
reverseboolFalseGlobe on the left.

When to use it

Use it for

  • Use it where the spread of the places is the point and each name leads to its own page.

Not for

  • A tour on a page that already moves; more than fourteen places; a second statement-sized globe (GusGlobe is the immersive one).

Behaviour

  • None: it is presentational.

Accessibility

  • The list is the content: each name a link; the globe and its label are aria-hidden.
  • Focus does what pointing does; every row is 44px or taller.
  • Reduced motion: the globe cuts instead of turning; the tour starts held.
