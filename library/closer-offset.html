Library / Closers and contact

Closer

Stable
LayoutLine CloserLineOffset CloserOffsetScene CloserScene

A workhorse closer for the Offset leaning: the closing line anchored low and left with its sentence and primary pill as one block, and one tall picture off to the right that leaves the grid at the window's edge; the block ends on the picture's foot and the picture gives the section its height. The picture drifts inside its frame. --reverse hangs the picture off the left edge. Below 960px: the picture first, edge to edge (3:2 on phones, 16:9 on tablets), then the line and the pill on the content edge, so the action is the last thing before the footer.

What it takes

ContentTakesDefaultNotes
titletextnoneA name over the line (a prize, a programme: ‘Next Up Founders Prize’). It becomes the section's h2 at h3 size (__title) and the line a display paragraph straight under it (__head, 12px): one block. Without it the line is the h2.
reversebooleanfalse--reverse: the picture on the left (columns 1 to 4 and the edge), the copy on columns 5 to 12: the same eight columns as the default, so a line breaks the same either way round.
long linesset by the helperautoA line longer than about 60 characters is a sentence: it is set at nu-heading--h2 whatever size was asked for (at display or h1 size it ran to four lines).
linetextrequiredAn h2 at nu-heading--display-s (--h1 for a longer line), three lines at most across eight columns.
leadtextnoneOne or two sentences, at most 52ch.
actions1–2 ButtonsrequiredThe closing action as a primary pill; an --outline alternative at most.
imageImage --fillrequiredOn columns 9 to 12 and out to the window's edge: 4:5, or --ratio-1x1, --ratio-3x2 (3:2 below 960px). --closer-offset-focus holds the subject.
reversebooleanfalse--reverse: picture on the left edge, copy on columns 6 to 12.
gaptight | standard | chapterstandardThe seam above, on the wrapper (nu-section--…). The page chooses it.
groundCanvas | Night | sunkenCanvasdata-mode="dark" on the wrapper for Night.

When to use it

Use it for

  • Use a mid-size picture here; the hero-scale one is higher up the page.

Not for

  • Use it straight after another section whose picture leaves the same edge.

Behaviour

  • click The pills' navigation.

Accessibility

  • The line is the section's heading; the picture is decorative unless it adds something.
  • Copy and picture each arrive once on scroll (data-reveal).
  • The picture drifts a few percent inside its fixed frame as the frame crosses the window (data-drift="soft"): a CSS scroll-driven animation, still where unsupported or under reduced motion. No text moves.

Markup

<section class="nu-section nu-section--bleed nu-closer nu-closer--offset" aria-labelledby="h">
  <figure class="nu-closer__media" data-drift="soft" data-reveal><img class="nu-image nu-image--fill" src="…" alt=""></figure>
  <div class="nu-closer__copy" data-reveal>
    <h2 class="nu-heading nu-heading--display-s nu-closer__line" id="h">Level up your AI initiatives by partnering with Northeastern</h2>
    <p class="nu-text nu-text--lead nu-closer__lead">…</p>
    <div class="nu-closer__actions"><a class="nu-button" href="…">Learn more {{i:arrow}}</a></div>
  </div>
</section>

Load nu-ds.css; patterns that move also need site.js and their pattern script. {{i:name} is an icon from the system set; {{photo:key} a photograph.
