FilmPlayer

Stable

A film that plays in place, in the square frame of whatever holds it. Loop: a muted film that plays while it is in view, with one glass pause toggle that is always there once script runs; the poster stands under reduced motion. Sound: the poster under one large play mark; on click the film plays where it is, with a glass bar for play, time, seek, sound, captions and full screen, and returns to its poster when it ends. The poster gives way to the film in a cross-fade and the two glyphs of each toggle cross-fade in place. The frame is always Night.

What it takes

ContentTakesDefaultNotes
kindloop | soundloopdata-dx-player on the root. Loop is decoration and stays muted; sound is content and never starts by itself.
src, posterurlrequiredThe poster is what shows without script, under reduced motion and before a sound film starts.
labeltextrequiredWhat the film shows: the video's accessible name, and the play button's (“Play the film: …”).
tracks[(src, srclang, label)] WebVTT filesnone<track kind="captions"> inside the video: tracks=[("video/coop-experience.en.vtt", "en", "English")] (a file under docs/assets/ or a URL). The captions button is in the bar only when the film carries a track; it turns the first track on and off (aria-pressed) and starts ON (the first track is default). Sound needs a track: kind="sound" with no tracks builds a muted click-to-play film (no sound or captions button, no native controls: nothing offers to unmute) and the build prints one warning naming the file. A film with speech needs one: the file is content and has to be transcribed, the player does not make it.
fillbooleantrue in the helper--fill: the player fills any positioned frame (absolute, inset 0): a hero's media frame, a stage, a band, a tab's picture frame. Without it the player is its own 16:9 frame.
focusobject-positionnoneThe focal point of the film and of its poster.
startsecondsnonedata-film-start: the second the picture begins, for a film that opens on black. A loop first starts there; a film with sound plays from 0 and keeps its poster until then.
controlbooleantrueThe loop's toggle sits bottom left of the frame; a holder moves it with --film-control-inset (an inset value). False leaves it out, to be placed elsewhere in the section (_dx.film_control(): beside a hero's pill).
driftnone | default | soft | strongnonedata-drift on the player's own frame (loops): the film and its poster drift, the controls do not.

When to use it

Use it for

  • Give every sound film a captions track: without one it plays silent.
  • Choose a poster that is a frame of the film.

Not for

  • Autoplay sound.
  • Put text on a loop without a Scrim (PictureBand does it for you).

Behaviour

  • play / pause data-film-state on the root: idle, playing, paused, ended. The toggle follows MediaControl: aria-pressed="true" means paused. data-film-playing fades the poster out: dx.js sets it once the film has shown a picture for --film-player-hold past data-film-start (and, where the frame can be read, not while it is still black), so a film that opens on black never cross-fades to a black frame. It is removed when a sound film ends.
  • in view A loop plays only while a quarter of its frame is in the window and nothing above it is inert or hidden (a tab panel that is not showing); it never plays under reduced motion unless the reader presses play.
  • drift The root directly holds the video and its poster, so a holder puts data-drift on it (MediaStage, PictureBand do, for loops): the film travels, the controls do not.
  • rest data-film-rest while a sound film plays and the pointer is still: the bar steps away, and returns on movement, focus or a key. It stays while the keyboard is in it.

Accessibility

  • The loop toggle is glass with a hairline ring (--film-control-ring): the dark disc reads on a bright frame, the ring on a dark one, 3:1 or better either way.
  • Every control is a real button with a fixed name; the seek line is a native range input with the time as its value text.
  • The play button covers the whole frame; on play, focus moves to the pause button in the bar.
  • A loop never plays under prefers-reduced-motion unless the reader presses play; it pauses when it leaves the window.
  • Without script a loop is its poster and a film with sound keeps its native controls and the designed ones stay hidden.
  • A film asked for with sound but given no captions track plays muted: its bar has no sound button, the video no native controls, and dx.js holds it muted; its play button reads “Play the film (no sound): …”.

Markup

<div class="nu-film-player" data-mode="dark" data-dx-player="sound">
  <video class="nu-video nu-film-player__video" playsinline controls preload="none" poster="…" aria-label="…"><source src="…" type="video/mp4"><track kind="captions" src="…" srclang="en" label="English" default></video>
  <img class="nu-film-player__poster" src="…" alt="" loading="lazy" decoding="async" data-film-poster>
  <button class="nu-film-player__play" type="button" aria-label="Play the film: …" data-dx-player-play hidden><span class="nu-film-player__mark">{{i:play}}</span></button>
  <div class="nu-film-player__bar" data-dx-player-bar hidden>
    <button class="nu-icon-button nu-film-player__toggle" type="button" aria-label="Pause the film" aria-pressed="false" data-dx-player-toggle>{{i:pause}}{{i:play}}</button>
    <span class="nu-film-player__time" data-dx-player-now>0:00</span>
    <span class="nu-film-player__seek"><span class="nu-film-player__fill" data-dx-player-fill></span><input class="nu-film-player__range" type="range" min="0" max="1000" value="0" aria-label="Seek" data-dx-player-seek></span>
    <span class="nu-film-player__time nu-film-player__time--total" data-dx-player-total>0:00</span>
    <button class="nu-icon-button nu-film-player__mute" type="button" aria-label="Mute" aria-pressed="false" data-dx-player-mute>{{i:sound-on}}{{i:sound-off}}</button>
    <button class="nu-icon-button nu-film-player__captions" type="button" aria-label="Captions" aria-pressed="false" data-dx-player-captions hidden>{{i:captions}}</button>
    <button class="nu-icon-button nu-film-player__full" type="button" aria-label="Full screen" data-dx-player-full>…</button>
  </div>
</div>

Load nu-ds.css; patterns that move also need site.js and their pattern script. {{i:name} is an icon from the system set; {{photo:key} a photograph.
