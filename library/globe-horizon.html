Globe

Stable
LayoutView GlobeViewOrbit GlobeOrbitHorizon GlobeHorizonTour GlobeTourChapters GlobeChaptersList CampusGlobe

A hero. The title and lead centred over the northern cap of a planet wider than the window, the campuses riding its horizon in the first screen. Scrolling (native; the stage is sticky) turns the globe west to east beat by beat, each beat's campus names set large top left; the last beat settles on a northern cap rising from the foot of the stage beside a figure. Any surface style. Reduced motion and no script: the planet at rest under the title, then every name.

When to use it

Use it for

  • Use one globe a page, where the reach of the network is the point; look from the north.

Not for

  • Lines between places (arcs, routes, connections); line-drawn land (hatching, outline-only coasts); any surface texture (grain, noise, paper, halftone, misregistration: Zach 11-13, "sleek and clean, not more random textures"); a globe cut by its container (GlobeHorizon's planet wider than the window is the one exception); a southern-hemisphere view; two globe patterns on one page.

Behaviour

  • None: it is presentational.

Accessibility

  • The names are the content: each a real link, in order, all in the tab order; the globe, its marks and labels are aria-hidden.
  • Focus does what pointing does; one current-state mark per list; focus rings on :focus-visible only.
  • Reduced motion: no drift, no flights, no pinning, no scrub; the complete still state.
