CampusAtlas

Stable
LayoutAtlas CampusAtlasWall MapWall

A workhorse. A flat map of North America (Albers equal-area) cropped to the set, a place across the Atlantic in a framed inset in the first clear corner; land a step off the ground, lakes cut out, borders as hairlines, edges fading into the ground. Each place an ink mark with its label, placed so that none collides (one that cannot stand clear is left off, never the chosen one). With script the names are a vertical tablist under the map (three columns, read down) and the chosen place shows a panel beside: square photograph, place, name, sentence, link; the chosen mark is the single red one, ringed. Phones: map, panel (picture beside copy), names in two columns. Without script: the map with every mark, then every place's name, sentence and link. When to use a map, and when the NameWall list: the list where people scan by name and a picture helps them choose; a map where breadth or geography is the point, or where the photographs are weak. CampusBand puts the geography into a NameWall's heading.
