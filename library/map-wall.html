CampusAtlas

Stable
LayoutAtlas CampusAtlasWall MapWall

A statement (once a page). On Night a quiet map of the whole set runs the full window (equidistant, true to scale east to west, so the ocean is as wide as it is), and the names stand as two columns of h3 links in its widest stretch of open water, clear of every place. Pointing at or focusing a name lights its place: the single red mark, ringed. Phones: the map across the window, then the names in two columns. When to use a map, and when the NameWall list: the list where people scan by name and a picture helps them choose; a map where breadth or geography is the point, or where the photographs are weak. CampusBand puts the geography into a NameWall's heading.
