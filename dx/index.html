What is experiential learning at Northeastern?

Every part of your journey at Northeastern is built for immersive learning, innovation, and integrating emerging technologies, like AI, to enhance creativity and career readiness.

  • Fully integrated from day 1

    Experience accelerated

    Your immersive learning experience starts the day you arrive—evolving with you and enriching your degree from beginning to end.

    Read the story

  • Unmatched opportunities

    Design your journey

    You’re in control of your journey with experiential options that span types, locations, and durations.

    Read the story

  • Learning without boundaries

    Go farther

    Study, work, and immerse yourself in culture, exploring new contexts and applying your academic knowledge.

    Read the story

  • Most expansive co-op program

    Co-op and lab research

    Gain full-time immersive professional work experience that positions you for a post-graduate job and network for your future.

    Read the story

  • Excel at top companies

    Projects with partnerships

    A global network of 3,900+ employer partners—spanning industries and continents—that continually evolves to provide real-world problem solving with exceptional companies.

    Read the story

  • Dedicated support at every step

    Faculty and advisor support

    Surrounded with professional guidance from day 1 to post-graduation, helping you choose the right opportunities for your academic journey.

    Read the story
