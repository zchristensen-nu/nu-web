The leader in shaping experiential learning for over 100 years

As the pioneer and architect of experiential learning since launching our co‑op program in 1909, Northeastern has spent more than a century cultivating embedded, career-aligned opportunities that give students not just work experience but a competitive edge shaped by continuous innovation.

More about co-op

Your ticket to global experiences

Where can Northeastern students work and learn internationally? Through our worldwide network of campuses, industry partners, and alumni. Each providing unique transformative global experiences that will enhance your education and shape your professional career path.

Global experiential network

Giving leading enterprises a competitive edge

We connect local and global enterprises with students who have the skills, leadership, and tech readiness—including responsible AI frameworks—to tackle both short-term projects and longer-term hiring needs. Learn how our experiential learning opportunities can benefit your organization.
